FAQ

Is NEET or CET mandatory?

No. Direct admission is available through ABS without NEET or CET for both D.Pharm and B.Pharm programs.

Are the colleges recognized?

Yes. All colleges are 100 % PCI-approved, many also AICTE & NAAC-A accredited.

Can I get a scholarship?

Scholarships up to ₹25 000 are available based on merit, need, or early application.

What’s better: D.Pharm or B.Pharm?

D.Pharm is a 2-year diploma; B.Pharm is a 4-year degree. We’ll help you choose the right path for your goals.

Placement support?

Yes, 100 % placement assistance. Alumni work at Cipla, Sun Pharma, Apollo Hospitals & more.
