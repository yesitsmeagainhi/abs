Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions About
Pharmacy Admission in Mumbai

Can I get direct D.Pharm admission in Mumbai without an entrance exam?
Yes! ABS Educational Solution helps 12th pass students get direct admission into government-approved pharmacy colleges across MMR. No entrance exam required — your 12th marks are sufficient to apply.
What is the fee for D.Pharm in government-approved colleges?
D.Pharm fees at government-approved pharmacy colleges start from ₹45,000 per year. ABS ensures complete fee transparency with zero hidden charges. Girl students may also be eligible for scholarship up to 100% on fees.
Is scholarship available for D.Pharm students in Maharashtra?
Yes, girl students can receive scholarship up to 100% on pharmacy fees through government schemes. ABS counsellors guide you through the complete scholarship application process as part of the free counselling session.
How does ABS Educational Solution help with pharmacy admission?
ABS provides: (1) Free counselling session to understand your goals, (2) College shortlisting based on your marks, budget, and location, (3) Full documentation support, and (4) Direct coordination with the pharmacy college for confirmed admission. 16+ years of experience, 16,000+ admissions done.
Which areas in Mumbai does ABS Educational Solution cover?
ABS has 6 branches covering the entire Mumbai Metropolitan Region: Bhayandar, Andheri, Thane, Nalasopara, Malad, and Kurla. You can visit your nearest branch for walk-in counselling or apply online for a call-back.
How long does D.Pharm take to complete?
D.Pharm (Diploma in Pharmacy) is a 2-year course after 12th standard. After completing D.Pharm, you are eligible to work as a registered pharmacist, work in hospitals, open a medical store, or pursue B.Pharm for higher studies.
